# BitGarth 0.1.9

## Highlights

- The new Holdings Report shows how every wallet moved over a date range you
  choose — opening value, closing value, and the total change, lined up in one
  view.
- Wallet sync status now leads with the issue that matters most, and one click
  opens the exact sync record behind it.
- Hledger exports have their own settings view, with account-prefix controls
  that match how your ledger is named.

## New

- Open the Holdings Report from the Reports section to compare wallet values
  across a full year or a custom date range.
- Name an account when you add it. Every add-account form — xpub, Bitcoin
  address, Ethereum address, and manual asset — now takes an optional name, and
  keeps auto-naming it for you if you leave the field blank.
- Set an account prefix in the new hledger export settings before you export.
- Add a price override on the free tier when a report is missing the price for a
  date.

## Fixed

- Historical price lookups are steadier over daily ranges, and now back off
  instead of retrying hard when a provider asks BitGarth to slow down.
- Long sync error messages stay fully readable — no more clipped or overflowing
  rows — and each sync row opens straight to its record.
- The Holdings Report counts your manual assets alongside synced wallets, so the
  totals reflect everything you hold.

## Notes

- On the free tier the Holdings Report always covers a rolling nine-month
  window. Upgrade to see your full history.
