# BitGarth 0.2.0

## Highlights

- Upgrading now starts your transaction history straight away, and the account
  page tells you what it is doing — "Syncing your ~N transactions…" — until the
  history is in.
- Every account page opens with an identity strip: what kind of account it is
  and which xpub or address it tracks, without a click.
- The Payments page always shows the plan you are on, even while the plan list
  is loading or unavailable.

## New

- See an account's type and its xpub or address at the top of its transactions
  page. Expand the strip for the full reference, a copy button, and the address
  list — the actions that used to hide in the kebab menu.
- Tell two accounts on the same xpub apart on the Wallets page: each row now
  carries its address type and a shortened reference.
- Read a plain-language summary of what each plan includes at the top of the
  Payments page, with the free tier priced as "$0 free".
- Reach us from the sidebar: an email address and a PGP public key for anything
  you would rather send encrypted.
- Get an answer from the sync button every time you press it, including
  "Already up to date (checked 17:51)" when there was nothing to fetch.

## Fixed

- An account with no synced history now shows an opening balance of "Not
  available" instead of a made-up number.
- Sync status settles correctly right after an upgrade.
- The Wallets and transactions pages show the same sync mark for the same
  account, so the two views no longer disagree.
