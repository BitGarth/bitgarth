## How to sign

This agreement is a non-exclusive licence, so no notarised deed, witness, or
handwritten signature is required. Sending the completed section below by email
forms the agreement.

1. **Send it from the same email address as Your Git commits.** This is what
   links the agreement to Your actual contributions. If You have already
   contributed under a different address, list both.
2. **Email the completed text to `hello@bitgarth.app`** with the subject
   `BitGarth CLA`.
3. **Optional but appreciated: sign the email with PGP.** Our key is published
   at <https://bitgarth.app/hello-bitgarth-pubkey.asc>.
4. **Comment on the pull request or issue** to say You have sent it, so that
   Your GitHub identity and Your email identity are linked.

We will reply to acknowledge receipt. Keep that reply — it is Your record that
the agreement is on file. Nothing will be merged until we have acknowledged.

**If You are under 18**, a parent or legal guardian must sign on Your behalf.
Have them complete the section below with their own details and note Your name
and GitHub username in the email.
