# Contributing

BitGarth's source is published so that the people who trust it with wallet data
can verify what it does. It is not published to crowdsource development.

**Pull requests are not accepted by default.** This is a source-first project
with no support SLA and a single maintainer. Please do not open a pull request
without asking first — it will most likely be closed unread, which wastes your
time more than ours.

## Reporting a security vulnerability

Do not use public issues, discussions, or pull requests. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Reporting a bug or suggesting a change

Open an issue describing the behaviour you saw and the behaviour you expected,
or email `hello@bitgarth.app`. Include your BitGarth version.

Please describe the problem rather than attaching a patch. Code posted in an
issue is subject to the same rules as a pull request, and an unsolicited patch
usually makes it *harder* for us to fix the bug ourselves — see below.

## If we do accept a contribution

Occasionally a proposed change is worth taking. In that case, before anything is
merged, the contributor must sign the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

We ask for this because BitGarth is licensed under the
[Functional Source License](LICENSE.md), which converts each version to Apache
2.0 after two years, and FernTrail B.V. needs to remain able to change the
project's licensing in future. Without a signed CLA, every contributor would
hold a veto over that. The CLA does **not** transfer your copyright — you keep
it, and you grant FernTrail B.V. a licence broad enough to relicense the work.

To sign: read [CLA.md](CLA.md) and follow the "How to sign" section at the end.
It takes one email. One signature covers all of your future contributions.

Contributions merged without a signed CLA on file are a mistake on our part, and
we will remove them.
